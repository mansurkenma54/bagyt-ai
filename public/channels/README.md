# Channel brand assets

These local assets identify the external channels shown in Bagyt. Their use does
not indicate an active connection, partnership, or endorsement. Brand ownership
remains with the respective companies.

Retrieved on 2026-10-15:

- `kaspi.svg` — unmodified official Kaspi favicon, linked from the Kaspi homepage:
  https://kaspi.kz/favicon/icon.svg
  Reference: https://kaspi.kz/
- `tiktok.png` — unmodified `TikTok_Icon_Black_Square.png` (1200 × 1200) from the
  official TikTok developer logo pack. The component applies a rounded display
  crop to the square background; the logo geometry and colors are unchanged.
  Guidelines: https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/getting-started-design-guidelines
  Pack: https://sf16-va.tiktokcdn.com/obj/eden-va2/uvzhqeh7nuhd/tt4d/logo-pack.zip
  Archive path: `Dev Portal Logo Pack/TikTok Logo Pack/TikTok – Icons/TikTok_Icon_Black_Square.png`

`ChannelIcon` serves these assets locally, with explicit dimensions, the channel
name as its accessible label, and the official contrasting backgrounds intact.
